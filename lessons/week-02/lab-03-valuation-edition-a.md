## AI boundary

Do not use generative AI during this lab until the unchanged Edition A has been submitted
and its Brightspace receipt verified. The session opener's tools-alive check — open the tool,
confirm it launches, type nothing — is launch verification, not use. Ordinary search, filings,
databases, spreadsheets, and calculators are allowed and must be identified.

## Required work

1. Locate the current primary filing (10-K or 10-Q) for the provisional Week 1 target.
   **Start here:** [SEC EDGAR full-text search](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/) — type
   the ticker, filter form type `10-K`; the newest annual report is your filing.
2. Record entity, filing/accession, reporting period, filing date, currency, units, diluted
   share evidence, cash, debt, and one operating-value driver — all from that filing.
3. Complete the synthetic enterprise-to-equity bridge check (known answer, done in class).
4. Write Project 1 Edition A covering, in any order and format that is yours: the decision
   and its user · company and valuation date · initial thesis (initiate / watch-defer / do
   not initiate) · known evidence with sources · consequential assumptions · unknowns · your
   research plan. That list is the whole requirement — no other document governs it.
5. Add one partner-generated falsification question without letting the partner write the
   answer.
6. Submit your Edition A as a **markdown file (.md)** — or another textual, AI-friendly
   format — to the separate Project 1 Edition A checkpoint, verify the receipt, and preserve
   the same file unchanged. Then record the receipt identifier in this lab checkout.
