Why data integrity is the first audit

Before anyone trusts your valuation, they check your numbers — where each came from, what it means, what you did to it. That is where models actually fail.

The contract: what object · what unit · what as-of date · what evidence. Break a clause and the number is noise.

Paired policy defense — 3 minutes each

Partner A first. Walk your partner through your nine lines — not what your criteria are, why they are yours: why this decision user, why this measure, why that disqualifier. Partner B: ask one "why" you don't buy. Then swap.

The shape, not your answer (training case): "My user is a junior analyst proposing a coverage add, so the screen has to survive a committee, not a hunch. I ranked on three-year volatility because that user gets one shot at defending the pick. And I disqualify any company without a current filing — everything I build in Weeks 2–4 has to read out of that filing."

Why: the nine lines were the easy part. A committee buys the reasoning behind them.

Gap check — what can you explain with an example?

  1. Enterprise value vs equity value vs per-share
  2. FCFF — free cash flow to the firm
  3. The enterprise-to-equity bridge

Every "not yet" goes on your written gap list — two lines each.
