# Lab 04 — Know Your Company: Deep Read and First Report

**Category:** Thursday merit checkout, 0–25.

**You arrive with:** your Lab 03 `.md` file · your downloaded 10-K/10-Q · your Lab 03 AI
chat, resumed — the context you built Tuesday is the point.

## In class

1. **Deep-read your filing — MD&A first.** 10-K: Item 7, "Management's Discussion and
   Analysis." 10-Q: Item 2. Lost your file? Start here:
   [SEC EDGAR full-text search](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/).
2. **Tell your partner the company** — 3 minutes each way, from your reading: what it is ·
   what is good and what is bad · challenges, risks, advantages · recent performance
   against its own history · the outlook, if management gives one. Swap.
3. **Rewrite your report with AI — resume your Lab 03 chat.** Widen the sources beyond one
   filing: two or three prior 10-Ks (same EDGAR page), recent news, a CEO interview or
   earnings call. Every claim keeps its source. End the report with your current call —
   **initiate / watch-defer / do not initiate** — and one sentence on what would change it.
4. **Organize your files — done together, projected.** One company = one folder, clear
   names with dates. The shape, not your answer:
   `AAPL-research/` → `Apple_2026-09-03_report.md` · `sources.md` · anything else you made.
   (Your company, today's date.) No `final_v2_REAL.md`, no `Untitled(3).md`.
5. **Publish your report** to your GitHub repository and to Substack — with the **report
   footer below** pasted at the bottom and filled in. *Publishing visibility is always
   your choice: submitting the same report file in the checkout earns identical credit.*

## Report footer — copy, paste, fill in

Every report you publish in this course ends with these three lines. They protect you,
and honest labeling is part of the craft:

> *This report was written for FIN 43900 (AI Finance Applications, Purdue) as a learning
> exercise. It is not investment research and it is not financial advice.*
>
> *AI assistance: drafted with [name your tools — e.g., ChatGPT/Codex], resumed from my
> Lab 03 session; sources gathered and verified by me; the judgments are mine.*
>
> *Any remaining errors are my own.*

**If network or a provider fails:** do the read, the partner story, and the rewrite
offline in your `.md`; publish after class; record the **exact failure** in your checkout.
The printed **Week 2 Offline/No-Code Fallback — Valuation Object and Bridge** remains the
no-network last resort.

## Merit anchors (for reference only) — 5 points each

| Criterion | 5 | 3 | 0–1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company understanding | the partner story covers what it is, good/bad, risks, performance vs. history, outlook — in the student's own words | present, one area thin | reads AI text aloud; no ownership |
| Source depth | filing plus at least two added sources, each cited where used | sources added but weakly tied to claims | single-source or uncited rewrite |
| Report quality | clear, organized `.md`; claims sourced; honest about unknowns | readable with gaps | disorganized or unsourced |
| AI use and disclosure | chat resumed; AI role disclosed; disclaimer present | AI used; disclosure thin | undisclosed or wholesale paste |
| Judgment | the good/bad/risk calls are the student's and defensible | judgments present but generic | nothing beyond the filing's own words |

Intermediate anchors: **4** = one minor weakness; **2** = an important link incomplete;
**1** = minimal attempt; **0** = missing or fabricated.
