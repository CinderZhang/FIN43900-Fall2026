# Session 3 — The Valuation Contract
**Week 2 · Tuesday · Lab 03: Project 1 Valuation Edition A**

---

## Why data integrity is the first audit (5 min)

Before anyone trusts your valuation, they check your *numbers* —
where each came from, what it means, what you did to it.
That is where models actually fail.

---

## Three outputs, never confused (10 min)

| Output | Meaning | Classic error |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise value | the operating business, all capital providers | comparing EV to market cap |
| Equity value | what shareholders own after the bridge | forgetting cash or other claims |
| Per-share | equity ÷ **diluted** shares | mixing share counts |

From the instructor video: three lenses (reverse DCF, pro-forma, multiples),
one honest range — that is Project 1's shape.
