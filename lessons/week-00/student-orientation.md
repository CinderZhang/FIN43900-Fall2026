# Welcome to FIN 43900

This course operates as a finance training camp. In most meetings you will receive a role, an
imperfect evidence set, and a decision. You will build, challenge, and defend an analysis—not
watch code being completed for you.

Most sessions follow: mission → human baseline → build → adversarial validation → decision →
checkout. Some work begins without generative AI so your initial reasoning is visible; other
work uses Codex and Gemini as competing analysts, implementers, or red teams. You remain
responsible for every source, number, convention, test, and recommendation.

DRIVER is one useful scaffold: **D**efine the decision and discover evidence, **R**epresent the
problem, **I**mplement an analysis, **V**alidate it, **E**volve it after testing, and **R**eflect on
the decision and evidence. You will also use valuation triangulation, the 5Cs of credit,
portfolio mandates, point-in-time data audits, falsification tests, model-risk registers, and
investment-committee memos.

For each major project, **Edition A** is your dated human baseline before project-specific
generative-AI research or coding. **Edition B** is the final, evidence-traceable version after
permitted AI assistance, testing, and revision. The two editions show how the analysis changed;
they are not two separate graded projects.

Route** in Start Here. If you cannot use an accessible free interface or the current task's
published packet, put `COURSE-RUN PROBE:` plus your exact question and prediction in the private
Brightspace response named by the activity; the instructional team runs it without using your
