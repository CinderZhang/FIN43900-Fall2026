# Support and Communication Map

| Need | First route | Bring |
|---|---|---|
| Course concept or instruction clarification | Brightspace Q&A forum | Exact page/lab and your interpretation |
| Personal, grade, accommodation, or sensitive matter | Private instructor channel listed in syllabus | Concise context; no sensitive public post |
| Environment/debugging | Lab/office hours with Beckett, Edward, or instructor | Error text, command, environment, attempted fixes |
| Finance/model judgment | Office hours or in-class committee probe | Decision, evidence, convention, and uncertainty |
| Broken Brightspace link/access | Course support route | Page title, time, device/browser, screenshot without secrets |
| Immediate credential exposure | Private instructor/security route | Secret type and repository/tool; never paste the secret itself |

TAs diagnose and probe; they do not complete code, approve recommendations, or conduct deep
debugging by email.
