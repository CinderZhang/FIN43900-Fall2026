# About Your Instructor

Dr. Xinde (Cinder) Zhang is an award-winning finance professor, curriculum innovator, and
leading voice in AI-integrated finance education. At Purdue University's Mitch Daniels School
of Business, he applies interdisciplinary experience in finance, computer science, and systems
design to how future finance professionals learn.

He created the DRIVER Framework: Define & Discover, Represent, Implement, Validate, Evolve,
and Reflect. His work integrates finance reasoning, Python, and large language models while
keeping evidence, validation, and professional judgment central.

Dr. Zhang's research examines frontier applications of AI in finance, including generative-AI
systems for financial analysis. Before Purdue, he founded the Financial Analytics concentration
at the University of Arkansas and mentored students into roles across investment banking,
asset management, consulting, payments, and technology. His teaching connects financial theory
to reproducible modeling and decision defense.
