# Student Handout — Integrated Capstone

## Role and deliverable

You are the lead analyst. Recommend both (1) the current finance/investment action and conditions
and (2) whether the committee should approve, revise, defer, or reject the system for decision
use. Leave Week 15 with a complete evidence graph, interface-risk finding, end-to-end test, top-
three failure budget, cold-run record, consistent submission bundle, and committee ask.

## Prework

Use **2–3 hours from Nov. 29–Dec. 1** to assemble reused components into an integration skeleton
and locate the current component outputs, README, memo/deck, and validation evidence. Write the
committee decision, name the issuer/corporate and investment components, and identify the material
input or state exchanged between them. Reserve the final 30 minutes for an AI-off prediction of
one entity/date/currency/unit/frequency/price/scenario/version interface that could fail.

Between Tuesday and Thursday, use **2–3 hours from Dec. 1–3** to produce the first end-to-end
runnable README-only cold-run package and draft Edition B, memo/validation updates, and outlines
for all three videos. These phases come from the published 10–14 out-of-class project hours; they are not extra
assignments. No required course work is assigned during Thanksgiving Break.

You may repair and improve a reused Project 1 or Project 2 component for the capstone even if its
earlier project score was low. Preserve the prior artifact/commit, identify the capstone repair,
and retest the interface. This does not reopen or regrade the earlier project; it prevents prior
performance from becoming a permanent capstone access barrier.

## Capstone Edition A checkpoint

Your immutable Edition A window was **Thu Nov. 19, 6:00 p.m. ET–Tue Nov. 24, 1:30 p.m. ET**,
identical for both sections. Preserve the unchanged file and first successful Brightspace

## Studio 1 — evidence graph

### Model-risk transfer card

This self-contained card adapts SR 26-2 Sections III and VI as professional analogy; **no student
rereading is required**. It extends the Week 12 validation lens with model purpose/exposure,
common assumptions, aggregate risk, effective challenge, clear roles, inventory, documentation,
validation, and monitoring. The 2026 guidance applies primarily to covered banking organizations
and expressly excludes generative and agentic AI from its model definition; it is not a legal
mandate for this student project. Your question is narrower: **where do apparently independent
components share one hidden failure, and what evidence would expose it?**

Create one row per material recommendation claim:

| Claim | Decision use | Component | Source/as-of | Convention/units | Calculation/output | Test/result | Owner | Reversal trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |

Every committee claim must trace to a row. “The dashboard shows” is not a source, convention, or
test. Agreement among components sharing one stale input is not independent confirmation.

## Interface audit

Check ticker/entity, date/as-of, currency/scale, units/sign, frequency/annualization, clean/dirty
or other price basis, scenario definition, portfolio weight, data/model version, and ownership at
every exchange.

| Interface | Expected contract | Actual | Failure consequence | Repair/qualification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
|  |  |  |  |  |

Write an expected direction first, change one input at the beginning of the pipeline, and trace
the effect through both components to the action. Ask AI for one contradiction/shared-dependency
test, then independently verify it before repairing, qualifying, removing, or deferring a claim.

Retain as **formative capstone working evidence**: evidence graph, one interface finding, and
end-to-end test. It creates no separate grade item.

Complete the separate **Changed-Input Return — Fresh Case, AI Closed** during the assigned
15-minute window. Preserve the prediction and result for learning evidence, but do not use the
formative window to repair the submitted capstone or create a new graded artifact.

## Three-video evidence

- Video 1: research evolution, attribution of change, one tested AI output, and why the conclusion changed or held.
- Video 2: code/data architecture, finance logic, and highest-risk changed-input result.
- Video 3: formal committee presentation—action/range, conditions, risk, trigger, and ask.

Practice a no-AI answer to one skeptical data, finance, validation, mandate, or reproducibility
question. AI may improve structure only after every accepted edit is tagged and rechecked.

Retain as **formative capstone cold-review evidence**: cold-run log, failure budget, A/B
reconciliation, and committee ask. It creates no separate points but belongs in the final
validation evidence.

## Common submission-integrity checkpoint

Complete this in Week 15, before the Week 16 consultation studios:

- repository and visible output open from a logged-out browser or second account;
- README-only cold run reaches the named visible output;
- memo/deck, Video 3, manifest, validation record, and output agree on the action and material numbers;
- each material number has a source/as-of/definition or explicit assumption label;
- all three video links open without owner credentials and corrected transcripts are present;
- filenames and links are descriptive, tables/figures have text explanations, and color is not the only signal;
- no credentials, private prompts/data, student records, or licensed data are exposed; and
- disposition is `GO`, `GO WITH NAMED LIMITATION`, or `NO-GO/CLAIM WITHDRAWN`.

## Capstone delivery — recorded submission and consultation studios

There is no live board and no scheduled defense. The capstone is delivered as the recorded
submission bundle defined on the Capstone assignment page: repository, problem statement then
and now (Editions A/B), three face-on-camera YouTube videos with transcripts enabled, and the
launch-post draft. The Week 15–16 class meetings are consultation studios — formative working
sessions where the teaching team pressure-tests your evidence and submission mechanics. Studios
create no points and no penalties; only the final submitted bundle is graded, to the common
deadline on the assignment header.

In Week 16, every student repeats only the credential-free link and Brightspace-receipt
confirmation; the full checkpoint is not reassigned, and that confirmation creates no new
graded requirement.
