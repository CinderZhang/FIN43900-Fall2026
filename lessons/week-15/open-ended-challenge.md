# Open-Ended Challenge — Components Agree for the Wrong Reason

Valuation, credit, and portfolio components all support the recommendation, but two consume the
same stale input and the third was calibrated to the resulting price.

1. Without AI, draw the dependency graph and mark circular/shared evidence.
2. Ask AI for three “independent confirmations”; reject any that reuse the same information.
3. Design one genuinely disconfirming test using a new source, vintage, convention, or
   precommitted changed input.
4. Close AI and state what remains valid, what must be withdrawn, and the smallest committee
   action while evidence is unresolved.

Deliver the dependency map, false-confirmation diagnosis, decisive test, bounded action, and
reversal trigger. Three agreeing outputs are not three pieces of evidence when their errors are
correlated.
