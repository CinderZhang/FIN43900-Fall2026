# Week 8 — Project 1 Due and Portfolio Mandate

## Mission

Tuesday is Fall Break: no class. Thursday's class is a guest talk; Lab 13 is done on your own
after it. The lab turns a client's words into rules a computer can check, before anyone builds a
portfolio. That is where Project 2 starts.

## You arrive with

- Project 1 nearly finished: submit it in the canonical Project 1 final assignment, the same one
  introduced in Week 2 ([the Project 1 page](../../projects/project-1-corporate-finance-application.md));
- your Course/Work Folder, VS Code and Python, and your AI partner.

**GitHub is the main delivery from now on.** Every lab and project is handed in as GitHub links to
your own files; the Brightspace quiz or assignment only collects the links.

## Outcomes

- Sort a client's statements into objectives, hard constraints, preferences, unverifiable
  promises and what is missing.
- Build a mandate checker from an instruction and prove it on a known answer.
- Explain why a portfolio can pass every checkable rule and still be wrong for the client.
- Name the one question the client must answer before anyone optimizes.

## Prerequisites and prework

Before Thursday, read the **Introduction** (page 1) and **Section 3, "Investment, Return, and Risk
Objectives"** (pages 8–15) of CFA Institute's free paper
[*Elements of an Investment Policy Statement for Institutional Investors*](https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/sites/default/files/-/media/documents/article/position-paper/investment-policy-statement-institutional-investors.pdf)
(about 15 minutes; a free PDF, no account). Ask: **which client fact becomes an
objective, which becomes a constraint, and which needs a question back to the client?**

Then come to Thursday's guest talk. Lab 13 is done on your own after the talk; it uses the
**assigned common case** (the fictional Meridian Foundation).

After the lab, write your own Project 2 mandate in the same shape; Project 2 starts from it in
Week 9.

## DRIVER this week

Define: the same question for everyone — which portfolios does the Meridian mandate permit, and
what must the CIO answer first? Represent: the CIO's statements, sorted. Implement: the checker
from one request. Validate: the known answer and the long-only probe. Evolve: your AI attacks the
checker. Reflect: why passing is not the same as suitable.
