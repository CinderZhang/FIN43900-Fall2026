# Lab 13 — Portfolio Mandate Defense

**Category:** Thursday merit checkout, 0–25 points.

## Required work

Complete the mandate before optimization: user/action, investable universe, horizon/rebalancing,
benchmark, measurable objective, risk/loss tolerance, liquidity, leverage/shorting,
concentration/turnover/suitability constraints from **Week 8 Common CIO Case — Meridian Foundation
Reserve**, data/as-of boundary, naive baseline, perturbation,
holdout logic, and failure condition. Compare two AI interpretations against the supplied CIO
facts and escalate one unresolved ambiguity.

For any criterion, use these intermediate anchors: **4** = sound with one minor,
non-load-bearing weakness; **2** = relevant evidence but an important link is incomplete;
**1** = minimal relevant attempt; **0** = missing, inaccessible, nonresponsive, or fabricated.

| Criterion | 5 — strong observable evidence | 3 — usable but incomplete | 0–1 — absent or unreliable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decision definition | user, action, horizon, universe, and decision timing explicit | one boundary vague | generic “best portfolio” |
| Objective and benchmark | measurable tradeoff and appropriate benchmark/naive baseline | objective usable but incomplete | maximize return without risk/user |
| Constraints and evidence | at least four operational constraints tied to facts; data/as-of boundary clear | constraints present with weak justification | invented preferences or vague limits |
| Validation design | perturbation, holdout, benchmark, cost, and failure conditions precommitted | several valid tests but one gap | validation chosen after results |
| Judgment and transfer | AI interpretations dispositioned; ambiguity escalated; decision reversal fact named | usable defense with vague escalation | AI/optimizer chooses preferences |
