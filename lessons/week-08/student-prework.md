Before Thursday, read the **Introduction** (page 1) and **Section 3, "Investment, Return, and Risk
Objectives"** (pages 8–15) of CFA Institute's free paper
[*Elements of an Investment Policy Statement for Institutional Investors*](https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/sites/default/files/-/media/documents/article/position-paper/investment-policy-statement-institutional-investors.pdf)
(about 15 minutes; a free PDF, no account). Ask: **which client fact becomes an
objective, which becomes a constraint, and which needs a question back to the client?**

Then come to Thursday's guest talk. Lab 13 is done on your own after the talk; it uses the
**assigned common case** (the fictional Meridian Foundation).

After the lab, write your own Project 2 mandate in the same shape; Project 2 starts from it in
Week 9.
