# Open-Ended Challenge — Mandate Collision

This is a changed transfer case after Lab 15. Its facts are not the Meridian Foundation common
case and must not be used as Lab 15 evidence.

The CIO requests a 7% annual return, no calendar-year loss greater than 5%, daily liquidity,
zero leverage, and at least 40% in short-duration government securities. Identify which parts
are preferences, constraints, aspirations, or unverifiable promises. Propose the minimum set of
questions required before analysis can responsibly continue.

Ask AI to propose a portfolio anyway. Tag every assumption it invents, then close AI and write the
smallest clarification set, provisional no-trade/analysis boundary, and evidence that would allow
the committee to proceed. Deliver a statement-classification table and conditional decision.
