DRIVER — your operating system

D · [ R · I · V · E ] · R — you dominate the edges, AI dominates the middle. 80/20 at every stage — never 100/0: you stay on the loop in the middle; AI still fetches for you at the edges.

DRIVER — your operating system
Stage In plain terms Iron Law
Define & Discover (~80% you) Own the question; inventory what you don't know No building without research first
Represent Plan the solution together with AI Don't reinvent what already exists
Implement Build it with AI as co-pilot Show, don't tell
Validate Check it against sources and known answers Trust but verify — especially with AI
Evolve Make the next cycle better Every cycle improves the next
Reflect (~80% you) Explain it in your own voice If you can't explain it on camera, you don't understand it

Reflect is how you are graded here

Validate the human, not just the machine.

Every project ends with you, on camera, explaining what you built, where AI contributed vs. where your judgment intervened, and what surprised you.

The artifact proves the AI worked. The explanation proves you did.

A screen can identify a company worth your research. It cannot prove an investment.
