## DRIVER — your operating system

**D · [ R · I · V · E ] · R** — you dominate the edges, AI dominates the middle.
**80/20 at every stage** — never 100/0: you stay on the loop in the middle;
AI still fetches for you at the edges.

| Stage | In plain terms | Iron Law |
|---|---|---|
| **D**efine & Discover *(~80% you)* | Own the question; inventory what you don't know | No building without research first |
| **R**epresent | **Plan the solution together with AI** | Don't reinvent what already exists |
| **I**mplement | Build it with AI as co-pilot | Show, don't tell |
| **V**alidate | Check it against sources and known answers | Trust but verify — especially with AI |
| **E**volve | Make the next cycle better | Every cycle improves the next |
| **R**eflect *(~80% you)* | Explain it in your own voice | **If you can't explain it on camera, you don't understand it** |

---

## Reflect is how you are graded here

**Validate the human, not just the machine.**

Every project ends with *you*, on camera, explaining what you built,
where AI contributed vs. where your judgment intervened, and what
surprised you.

The artifact proves the AI worked. **The explanation proves you did.**

A screen can *identify* a company worth your research. It cannot prove an investment.
