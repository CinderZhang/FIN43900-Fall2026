# Optional Open-Ended Challenge — Break Your Own Screen

This is a common ungraded extension, not a separate course track.

## Challenge

Create the smallest plausible change that causes your screening policy to select a different
company. You may change one—and only one—of these:

- lookback window;
- risk-free-rate assumption;
- one threshold or weight;
- treatment of adjusted versus unadjusted prices;
- one exclusion; or
- one missing-data rule.

Then answer:

1. Why is the changed input professionally plausible rather than arbitrary?
2. Which companies change rank, and through which metric?
3. Does the instability reveal a bad company, a weak metric, or an under-specified decision?
4. What additional primary evidence would you collect before locking the target?
5. Can you formulate a screen that is less fragile without hiding real uncertainty?

## No-AI transfer

After completing the challenge with AI assistance, close the AI systems. Explain to a partner
why robustness is not the same as correctness and why a stable ranking can still be based on a
bad definition. The partner may ask one follow-up question. Record the question you could not
answer immediately; that question becomes the next research task.
