Before Tuesday, complete the Brightspace **Start Here** onboarding module; watch the 31:07
[published DRIVER video](https://youtu.be/gUepTIp11Og); take the five-minute ungraded practice quiz; and spend about ten minutes
writing one investor question plus two provisional screening criteria without AI (rough
notes are enough — the full screening policy is written together in class).

**You do not set up any software before class.** All technical setup — Colab, ChatGPT,
Gemini, the readiness check — happens **together, step by step, in Tuesday's class**, with
the instructor projecting every click and TAs walking the rows. Your only technical prework
is what you bring: a charged laptop, your Purdue login, and the two AI subscriptions from
**AI Access — Required Subscriptions and the Fallback Routes** signed in on your phone or
laptop browser (sign-up help is also available in class — arriving without them costs you
nothing but a few minutes of class time). If you *want* a head start you may try the setup
at home, but it is optional, never assumed, and no grade ever depends on it working before
you arrive.

Do not pre-complete the live readiness evidence. The **academic Week 1 prework** is **45–55
minutes**: the 31:07 DRIVER video, five-minute practice quiz, ten-minute human screen, and
gathering the sign-ins above. Complete the separate **Start Here onboarding** before this
work. **Combined arrival budget: 90–130 minutes.** Stop at the published support boundary
rather than spending more; nothing technical is graded before Tuesday's guided setup.
When a starter fails, use the shared **Starter Help Ladder** from Level 0 upward; do not ask AI
for a completed file or repository.
Before Thursday, bring the Tuesday target, evidence record, and a working environment or named
remediation path — the environment you will have because Tuesday's class builds it with you.
