# Watch — DRIVER Framework and Provisional Target Company

Watch the published 31:07 video: [Learn the DRIVER Framework and Identify a Target
Company](https://youtu.be/gUepTIp11Og).

Its purpose is to establish the first human/AI cycle and show why a screening score begins an
investigation rather than proving an investment conclusion. Before class, answer without AI:

1. Which DRIVER stage keeps the target question from collapsing into a ticker screen?
2. What evidence could disqualify a high-scoring company?
3. Which one target fact must be verified in a filing or authoritative source?

If the player or automatic captions do not work, use the **Readable DRIVER alternative** in the
Week 1 student handout. It contains the six-stage reference and the complete assessed screening
exercise; it is not represented as a verbatim transcript. No assessed detail depends exclusively
on the video: the handout includes every concept checked by the practice quiz. This is a Week 1
instructional object and is not part of the syllabus.

You will learn and practice the DRIVER cycle explicitly in Week 1. Lab 01 can be one graded
demonstration under the published best-eight buffer; there is no hidden make-up or separate
DRIVER submission if that score is dropped. In later finance work, using the stage names is
optional; human framing and Reflect—validating your own judgment and ownership, not only the AI
output—are not. Valuation, credit, portfolio, provenance, and model-risk structures should govern
the finance answer.
