# Open-Ended Challenge — Cheap or Unreliable?

K4 has the largest supplied spread and the stalest, least-active observation.

1. Without AI, list the mutually competing explanations consistent with the table.
2. Ask AI for a “buy” case and identify every claim requiring data not supplied.
3. Design the minimum evidence request that distinguishes relative value from stale or illiquid
   measurement: fresh executable quotes, trade history, curve benchmark, issuer filings, capital
   structure/seniority, liquidity source, or another defensible item.
4. Close AI and decide reject, quarantine, or deeper review today.

Deliver a compact evidence request with decision owner, freshness standard, claim limit, and
reversal condition. Do not infer a rating, default probability, or recovery value.
