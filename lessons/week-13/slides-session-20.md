## I — the cold run, by your partner

**Minutes 5–25**

Something not working? Debug with your AI: the exact command and the exact error text.

1. Send your partner your repository link and nothing else.
2. They open it in their own folder, follow only the README, and write in your `.md` file: the
   command, the first failure, the elapsed time, where the result is.
3. Swap. Do not coach; a question you have to answer is a README line you have to write.

**At minute 20:** open the quiz attempt — Brightspace → Quizzes → Lab 20.

---

## V — one changed input, predicted first

**Minutes 25–40**

1. AI closed: pick one load-bearing input (an estimate, the lag, the cost). Before the run, write
   the old and new value with units, the direction you expect the decision numbers to move, and
   the effect you expect on the decision. Commit and push.
2. Rerun. Keep the before and after output. Under the prediction write what happened, whether the
   decision changes, and why the actual matched your prediction or not — or why nothing changed.

A changed-lag run is a sensitivity scenario: your original frozen rule and holdout stay the evidence.

*Example (Meridian, Lab 15):* "Equity return 8.0% → 7.0% a year; I expect the lowest-risk mix to
add equity and the decision to hold. Actual: 55 → 80 points of equity, volatility 9.44% → 12.94%.
Matched: a lower equity return needs more equity to reach the objective. Decision holds: pilot, not adopt."
