Session 6 — Attack the DCF

Week 3 · Thursday · Lab 06: Sensitivity, Reverse DCF, and Conditional Recommendation

Launch (12 min, instructor-led)

Prework Step 6 asked you to predict WACC 10% → 11% before running it.

Answer: $27.50 → $23.41 — a 15% drop from one point.

Direction right but size shocking? That is the lesson: this conclusion is hostage to the discount rate. The committee hears that as a range.

Watch one sensitivity cell built live; then it's your engine.

Scenario design is causal, not cosmetic (10 min)

A scenario is a coherent story: higher growth usually needs higher reinvestment; higher risk moves cash flows and the rate. Never combine mutually inconsistent "best" assumptions.

Your target, under attack (35 min)

  • WACC/g grid + one operating sensitivity, with monotonicity checks
  • Reverse DCF: what growth does today's price imply? Is it plausible?
  • AI bull/bear challenge: have each system attack your thesis; verify one claim

Temperature check (5 min, anonymous, ungraded)

Three questions, no names: what's working · what's confusing · one change you'd make. Answers get addressed openly next Tuesday.

Defend + checkout (8 + 5 min)
