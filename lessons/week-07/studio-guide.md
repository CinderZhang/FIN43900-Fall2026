# Week 7 Studio Guide — Project 1 Q&A and Consultation

*Both Week 7 meetings are open Q&A/consultation studios. There is no lab checkout and no lab
points this week — bring your Project 1 work and leave with answers. The instructor and TAs
are in the room as your knowledge experts.*

## How a studio session works

1. Come with your project open and one specific question, blocker, or claim to pressure-test.
2. Short whole-room open: common blockers we are seeing, answered once for everyone.
3. Open consultation: work, ask, verify. Questions get answered in the order they arrive;
   the whiteboard queue is first-come.
4. Anything answered for one team that matters to everyone gets re-broadcast to the room.

## Recommended self-audit before you ask (optional, ungraded)

The strongest consultation questions come from a failure audit you ran yourself first:

- State your committee decision and the highest-risk claim behind it.
- List your three most likely failure modes across finance, data, and technical.
- Complete a README-only cold run: fresh environment, your own setup instructions, note the
  first failure and the elapsed time.
- Ask AI for one concrete counterexample or test of your highest-risk claim; verify it
  independently; then repair, qualify, or reject the claim.

## Recommended defense rehearsal (optional, ungraded)

Practice the two-minute defense with a partner: 45 seconds on A-to-B evidence, 45 seconds on
the code and highest-risk test, 30 seconds on the committee decision — then take one skeptical
question. The Project 1 rubric criteria are exactly what a listener should probe: evidence
evolution, technical ownership, validation, investment judgment, response quality.
