# Student Handout — Project 1 Audit and Defense Studio

## Bring to class

Your unchanged Edition A, current Edition B, repository, environment file, visible executed
output, decision memo/deck draft, validation record, and draft manifest. Do not bring API keys
or private data. Confirm the instructional team can access a private repository.

Use the one shared file named `project-submission-manifest-template.csv`; there is no second Week 7
template. Fill and save a working copy rather than auditing the untouched blank download. Place
your completed working file beside `project_audit.py` and run:

`python project_audit.py project-submission-manifest-template.csv`

The audit checks mechanical completeness only. Read **Project 1 — Annotated Decision-Evidence
Excerpt and Non-Example** before the studio; it shows observable anchors, not a copyable answer.
If a terminal is unavailable, inspect the same 12 rows manually with a partner or staff member and
mark each missing field; this is the equivalent route and creates no extra grade item.

## Audit protocol

1. State the decision and highest-risk claim without opening the code.
2. List three ways that claim could be wrong: one finance convention, one data issue, and one
   technical or reproducibility issue.
3. Give a partner only your README. Record the first cold-run failure and time to visible
   output. Do not coach them.
4. Ask an AI system for a specific counterexample or changed-input test. Independently perform
   the arithmetic or source check before accepting it.
5. Repair, qualify, or reject the claim. Preserve the evidence.

## Three evidence surfaces

| Surface | Unique question |
|---|---|
| Video 1 | What changed or remained stable after project-specific research; what drove it; and what evidence justified it? |
| Video 2 | How does the submitted code produce the result, and what high-risk test passed or failed? |
| Video 3 | What should the committee do, under what conditions, and what would reverse the decision? |

The memo/deck and repository must stand alone. Videos demonstrate ownership; they do not
replace the work. Use the exact bundle and duration rules in the project architecture.
