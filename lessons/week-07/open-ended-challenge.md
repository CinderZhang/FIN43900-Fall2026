# Open-Ended Challenge — Break Your Own Recommendation

Construct the smallest plausible change in a source fact, convention, or operating assumption
that reverses your current recommendation. Explain whether that change is merely possible or
decision-relevant, how it would be monitored, and whether the committee should act now.

There is no single answer. A dramatic but implausible shock is weaker than a small, evidenced
boundary case.

Ask AI for the strongest countercase, verify its key fact or arithmetic independently, then close
AI and write the smallest committee action, monitoring owner, and reversal threshold. Preserve
the original recommendation and changed-case evidence in a compact before/after table.
