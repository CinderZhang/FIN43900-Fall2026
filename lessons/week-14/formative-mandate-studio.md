# Formative Studio — Fixed-Income Committee Decision

This studio creates no grade item after Lab 24.

## Required sequence

1. Submit no work yet: freeze the human mandate/K4 prediction.
2. Validate weights, portfolio duration, and qualifying liquid weight.
3. Compute separate rate and spread approximations and state what they omit.
4. Test duration, concentration, liquidity-weight, and no-add-stale rules.
5. Disposition the AI yield-pickup trade and one alternative/no-trade action.
6. Recompute affected constraints before making the committee decision.
7. Complete a no-AI explanation of current holding versus allowable addition.

## One-page exit record

Mandate and user · frozen prediction · known-answer reconciliation · separate scenario table ·
constraint report · AI accepted/rejected/qualified claims · proposed action/weights · evidence
request · approximation boundary · two monitoring/reversal triggers.

Success means convention-consistent arithmetic and mandate-owned action—not highest modeled
income. If the environment fails, the accessible table/calculator path is equivalent.
