# Open-Ended Challenge — Yield Pickup with a Mandate Breach

AI recommends shifting 25 percentage points into high-spread K4. Modeled income rises, but K4 is
stale and sparsely traded.

1. Without AI, identify each current and post-trade mandate test.
2. Ask AI to defend the trade and tag every invented objective, unsupported execution claim, or
   hard-constraint violation.
3. Propose the smallest compliant alternative: smaller/no trade, another bond, fresh-evidence
   gate, or a mandate clarification. Recalculate the affected constraints.
4. Close AI and issue the committee action with monitor and reversal trigger.

Deliver a short before/after constraint table and evidence request. A higher modeled yield cannot
override the no-add stale rule or create market liquidity.
