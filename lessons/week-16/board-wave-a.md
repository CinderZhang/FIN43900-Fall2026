# Tuesday — Committee Board Wave A

Students assigned to Tuesday report to the published room/board/slot. Each slot is six minutes:
three-minute decision defense, up to two minutes for one verbatim question from the disclosed
Common Pre-Board Challenge, and one minute
for transition/evidence recording.
Instructor, Beckett, and Edward run the three calibrated boards. Arrive with the frozen package
already accessible; do not edit the board snapshot during the board. The final capstone remains
governed only by its common deadline and rubric. Board observations are released after both waves
for professional development; they are not a revision opportunity and cannot cure or change the score.
Boards may ask only a neutral evidence-location clarification; they may not introduce a new fact,
undisclosed follow-up, or project-specific repair hint.

Students not assigned Tuesday are excused from the defense zone, complete the published access
check asynchronously, and do not observe, record, or share another student's work. If the operating gate
fails, the published zero-penalty Video 3/frozen-evidence fallback applies to everyone affected.
