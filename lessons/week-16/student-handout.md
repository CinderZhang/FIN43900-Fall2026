# Student Handout — Capstone Committee Defense

## Purpose and frozen-evidence rule

Your live defense is not a fourth presentation artifact or separate grade item. It is a concise
ownership/clarification check tied to a dated **board snapshot** of the repository, written
evidence, and Videos 1–3. The board snapshot is not `CAP-FINAL` and does not shorten the common
Dec. 10, 11:59 p.m. ET deadline. Only the final Brightspace submission and receipt govern grading.
A live answer cannot replace, edit, or cure unsupported evidence in that final submission, and
board observations are withheld until both waves close.

## Before your assigned board

- verify repository, visible output, memo/deck, Research-Evolution PDF, Validation-and-AI-Use PDF,
  three videos/transcripts, and manifest are accessible;
- repeat only the common Week 15 credential-free link and Brightspace-receipt confirmation; the
  full checkpoint is not reassigned in either Week 16 mode;
- confirm the same action, range, numbers, as-of dates, conditions, and triggers across surfaces;
- open **Week 16 Committee Board Schedule and Access Route** and read your section/day/board/slot
  only if that page says the board gate is active;
- prepare the one-card defense below; and
- before Tuesday's first board wave, practice and preserve an answer to **every exact Common
  Pre-Board Challenge question** below using frozen evidence. This is the same preparation event
  for Tuesday, Thursday, and recorded-defense students; it is not a submission.

Never display credentials, private data, or another student's information.

## Three-minute defense card

1. **Action and committee ask:** approve, revise, defer, or reject what, for whom?
2. **Range/allocation and conditions:** what should occur now, not a false-precision point?
3. **Strongest evidence and as-of date:** where is it in the dated board snapshot?
4. **Principal risk/assumption:** what most threatens the action?
5. **Reversal/kill trigger:** what observable evidence changes the decision?

Do not spend the defense on biography, course process, or a screen tour. Lead with the decision.

## Question response protocol

Pause, identify the claim, locate the evidence, state fact/assumption/output/uncertainty, and answer
the decision consequence. If the question reveals a material defect:

1. bound the affected claim;
2. state what remains valid;
3. withdraw or qualify unsupported conclusions;
4. recommend approve/revise/defer/reject; and
5. name the next decisive test and owner.

It is acceptable to say what the system cannot establish. Unsupported certainty is weaker than a
bounded, evidence-based answer.

## Common Pre-Board Challenge — disclosed to every student before Wave A

Prepare all five exact questions before Tuesday's first board wave:

1. **Data/as-of:** Which source and availability date support your most material input?
2. **Finance/convention:** Which convention drives your range, and what changes under the
   alternative?
3. **Validation/failure:** Where is the changed-input or contradiction test that could reverse
   your action?
4. **Mandate/user:** Which constraint permits this action, and what would make the committee defer?
5. **Implementation/reproducibility:** Where does the frozen code produce the visible result?

The live or recorded committee selects one of these five questions verbatim. It may ask only a
neutral location clarification such as “where is that evidence?” It may not introduce a new fact,
project-specific repair hint, or undisclosed challenge before the common final receipt. The board
records the quality of your response but withholds its observation until after the common deadline.
Because every cohort receives the full challenge set before Wave A, no wave receives an earlier
revision clue.

## Access and fallback

Tell the instructional team privately before your slot if the published schedule or format creates
an access barrier. If the course cancels parallel boards because of room, audio, network, staffing,
schedule, or access failure, your required Video 3 and frozen written/repository evidence govern
with no penalty. Follow only the published alternative; do not create an extra video or submission.

Before the common final receipt, both delivery modes permit the same private preparation: preserve
an AI-off baseline defense, answer all five disclosed Common Pre-Board Challenge questions,
optionally request one clarity critique, independently disposition it, and make at most one bounded
re-recording that corrects accuracy or decision usefulness. Boards and fallback peers use only the
already disclosed questions and do not release observations early, so neither wave nor mode receives
a new repair clue.

If you miss an activated individual slot, absence alone does not change the capstone score. Contact
the published support route; the instructor will schedule the same three-minute frozen-evidence
defense plus one material question within five business days in an accessible in-person or live-
remote format. The record remains `FORMATIVE MAKE-UP OPEN` until completion. No fourth video or
unpublished grade deduction is permitted.
