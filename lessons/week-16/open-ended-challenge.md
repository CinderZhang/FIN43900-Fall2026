# Open-Ended Challenge — The Board Rejects Your Model but Accepts Your Judgment

During questions, the committee identifies a material model defect.

1. Without consulting AI, identify the exact claim and downstream decisions affected.
2. State what evidence and components remain valid.
3. Withdraw or qualify unsupported conclusions rather than defending the model reflexively.
4. Recommend approve, revise, defer, or reject with the next decisive test, owner, and timing.
5. Afterward, ask AI for a stronger response and reject any statement that rewrites the frozen
   evidence or invents facts.

Deliver a 90-second response and a claim-impact map. The goal is evidence-bounded judgment, not
winning an argument or pretending the defect does not matter.
