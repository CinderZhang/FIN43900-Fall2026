## Practice protocol

Practice one answer each for data/as-of, finance/convention, validation/failure, mandate/user, and
implementation/reproducibility. For each answer name the evidence location and what the system
cannot establish. Then ask AI to critique clarity only; reject any invented fact, number, source,
or post-submission repair. Preserve the AI-off baseline and critique disposition. Before the
common final receipt, make at most one bounded re-recording only when it improves accuracy or
decision usefulness. Close AI and answer, without a script, one of the five disclosed questions
selected by a partner. No undisclosed challenge may be introduced.

Verify access and the published schedule/alternative route. This preparation creates no grade
item and cannot cure missing frozen evidence.
