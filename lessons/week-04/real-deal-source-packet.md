# Real-Deal Source Packet — Broadcom / VMware

Use this frozen source map for the in-class real-deal audit when a student's independently chosen
deal is not yet admissible. It is evidence infrastructure, not a completed precedent-transaction
multiple.

## Decision date and scope

- **Announcement date:** May 26, 2022.
- **Close date:** November 22, 2023.
- **Teaching question:** What did the buyer offer, what valuation object did each headline number
  describe, and which tempting denominator must be rejected?
- **Permitted use:** source reconciliation and transaction-context audit. Do not treat one deal as
  a peer set or as proof of value for the student's target.

All links were rechecked on **August 15, 2026**. SEC filings are public-access primary sources.
The Broadcom page is linked, not reproduced. This course packet paraphrases a small number of
transaction facts; students must cite the underlying page and may not upload or republish source
documents as though the course granted reuse rights.

## Known-answer boundary

1. The approximate announcement bridge is `$61 billion equity transaction value + $8 billion
   assumed net debt = $69 billion enterprise-value-like headline amount`.
2. Do **not** divide $69 billion by the announcement's approximately $8.5 billion of projected
   pro forma EBITDA. That figure describes the combined acquisition contribution expected within
   three years after closing, not VMware LTM EBITDA at announcement.
3. A defensible precedent multiple requires a separately sourced, date-aligned target denominator
   with a stated reported/adjusted convention. If that evidence is unavailable, record the deal's
   terms and context but exclude its multiple from the valuation range.

## Student audit

Before AI, predict which of these changes can alter the implied consideration: Broadcom's share
price, proration, net-debt estimate, target denominator, or closing status. Then verify the three
sources, label every value as equity, debt, enterprise-like, or denominator, and decide **use,
qualify, or exclude**. AI may help locate definitions after the prediction; it may not invent the
missing denominator.
