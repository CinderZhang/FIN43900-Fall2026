# Session 7 — Comparable Means Defensible
### Week 4 · Tuesday · Lab 07: Comparable-Company Policy and Implied Range

---

## When your DCF says $27 and the market says $19 (7 min)

Somebody is wrong. Multiples are how you find out who — the prices real
buyers pay for similar businesses, right now.
But "similar" is a judgment hiding inside a word.

**Policy before peers** — commit to criteria before you see who passes.

---

## The worked case, one peer by hand (10 min)

> Alpha EV/EBITDA = 1,200 / 120 = **10.0×**

| Peer | EV/EBITDA | P/E |
|---|---|---|
| Alpha | 10.0× | 12.0× |
| Beta | 16.0× | 20.0× |
| Gamma | 9.0× | 15.0× |
| Delta | 16.0× | 15.0× |

9× to 16× for "comparable" companies — the dispersion IS information.

---

## Apply — and the trap (10 min)

> Median EV/EBITDA 13× → EV 1,040 → **bridge** → 790 → **$15.80**
> Median P/E 15× → equity 600 directly → **$12.00**

**Enterprise multiples need the bridge; equity multiples already crossed it.**
Bridging a P/E answer double-counts the capital structure.
