Why does the deal sit above trading? Control, synergies, an auction, a moment in time. Why is averaging the three numbers malpractice? The spread is the finding.

Deal evidence has a timestamp (10 min)

From the real-deal packet: use only what was knowable at announcement. Closing evidence answers status; it does not make announcement-date inputs retroactively knowable. Defend the deal's context or set it aside.

Triangulate your company (35 min)

  • Put your DCF range and your comps range side by side
  • Reconcile against the deal evidence you qualified
  • Explain the disagreements — each method prices something different
  • Remove or normalize one item; watch what moves

The 45-second recommendation (10 min)

Formal, to a partner acting as committee: action · range · the evidence that best fits the question · condition · reversal trigger. Then answer one skeptical question.

Checkout (8 min)

Method comparison + explained disagreement · qualified deal evidence · 45-second recommendation content · AI paths · in-person attendance declaration · truth attestation · receipt.
