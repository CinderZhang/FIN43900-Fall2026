# Lab 08 — Deal Evidence and Valuation Triangulation

**Category:** Thursday merit checkout, 0–25.

**If code, network, or deal-data access fails:** open **Week 4 Offline/No-Code Fallback —
Comparable Policy and Triangulation** immediately. Complete its manual synthetic route, submit
the same checkout fields, and record the exact failure and synthetic-case label. A live deal
claim still requires dated, verified evidence.

## Required work

1. Reproduce the synthetic precedent-transaction implied value.
2. Audit one real deal or the supplied Broadcom/VMware source packet: terms, dates, status, value bridge, denominator,
   control/synergy context, and comparability.
3. Put DCF, trading comparables, and deal evidence into the triangulation table.
4. Run one changed-inclusion or changed-normalization test.
5. For the no-current-position committee, recommend a range and initiate/watch-defer/do-not-
   initiate or deeper-research action with a condition and trigger.

non-load-bearing weakness; **2** = relevant evidence but an important link is incomplete;
**1** = minimal relevant attempt; **0** = missing, inaccessible, nonresponsive, or fabricated.
