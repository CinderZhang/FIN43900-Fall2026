# Open-Ended Challenge — The Concentrated Optimum

An unconstrained model assigns 87% to the asset with the highest estimated mean and reports an
excellent in-sample Sharpe ratio.

1. Without AI, list at least three competing explanations: genuine opportunity, mean-estimation
   error, covariance error, incompatible units, hidden objective, or solver/model defect.
2. Ask AI to propose the smallest intervention. Classify it as mandate-derived, statistical,
   cosmetic, or unsupported.
3. Design one test that distinguishes estimation error from a genuine opportunity.
4. Close AI and defend the smallest committee action that preserves learning without violating
   the mandate.

Deliver a one-page decision note: hypothesis, intervention, test, expected direction, result if
available, mandate effect, action, and reversal trigger. There is no single preferred cap or
method; arbitrary bounds chosen to make a chart attractive are weak evidence.
