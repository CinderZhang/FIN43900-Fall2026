# Lab 15 — Portfolio Stability Decision

**Category:** Thursday merit checkout, 0–25 points.

## Required work

Run at least three precommitted tests across mean/covariance/window, constraint, and cost. Include
one short no-AI changed-case decision. Recommend adopt, rebalance, pilot, or reject.

For any criterion, use these intermediate anchors: **4** = sound with one minor,
non-load-bearing weakness; **2** = relevant evidence but an important link is incomplete;
**1** = minimal relevant attempt; **0** = missing, inaccessible, nonresponsive, or fabricated.

| Criterion | 5 — strong observable evidence | 3 — usable but incomplete | 0–1 — absent or unreliable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem and mandate | decision user, action, horizon, benchmark, objective, and hard constraints govern the analysis | one boundary vague | generic “best portfolio” |
| Data and financial soundness | frequency/units, covariance, weights, bounds, and baselines reconcile | minor convention/check gap | mixed units, hidden shorting, or invalid weights |
| Validation and robustness | expected directions precede three material tests; gross/net and failures interpreted | valid but shallow tests | in-sample metric or AI agreement only |
| Portfolio judgment | action/range follows mandate and evidence; fragile input and condition are explicit | usable recommendation with vague condition | “optimal” treated as self-justifying |
| Explanation and transfer | claim dispositions, changed case, monitoring, and reversal trigger are defensible | mostly descriptive | copied output with no ownership |

## Individual checkout

Lab Date · teammates/learning partners/contribution · frozen candidate and baseline · test table with predicted and
actual directions · cost convention · mandate status · AI claim dispositions · no-AI transfer
answer · committee action/range/condition · monitoring and reversal trigger · optional link ·
session token (announced in the room) · in-person attendance declaration, truth attestation · receipt confirmation.

End your checkout with a **growth note** before you submit — two or three sentences,
**ungraded and for you alone**: what can you do now that you could not do before this
lab, and what still feels shaky? Nobody scores this. It is your own record of becoming
good at this, and by December it is the raw material your project Reflect videos are
built from.
