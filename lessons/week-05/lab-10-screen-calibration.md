Build an interpretable approve/review/reject screen for the synthetic borrower set. Preserve
factor reasons and override rules. Compare decisions with the supplied historical case labels,
identify false approvals/rejections, change one defensible threshold or missingness rule, and
recommend a calibrated policy plus escalation path.

For any criterion, use these intermediate anchors: **4** = sound with one minor,
non-load-bearing weakness; **2** = relevant evidence but an important link is incomplete;
**1** = minimal relevant attempt; **0** = missing, inaccessible, nonresponsive, or fabricated.

| Criterion | 5 | 3 | 0–1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem/decision | exposure, horizon, actions, and asymmetric error costs explicit | one boundary vague | generic risk score |
| Data/evidence | definitions, periods, units, missingness, labels, and sources trace | minor gap | untraceable ratios/labels |
| Validation | known answers, error review, changed policy, and override test interpreted | valid but shallow checks | accuracy/AI agreement only |
| Financial judgment | calibrated action, qualitative override, condition, escalation | usable with vague condition | score treated as underwriting truth |
| Explanation/transfer | factor reasons and error tradeoff defendable | mostly descriptive | opaque copied model |
