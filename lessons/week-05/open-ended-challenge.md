# Optional Open-Ended Challenge — Make the Model Argue With You

**Ungraded. Never collected. Nobody reads this.** The in-room floors this week were Tuesday's ABG
known answer reproduced with the refusal shown, and Thursday's labelled assumption set, balancing
statements and one value for your company. This page is what to do with the rest of the week if
you want to be the person in the room who has actually done it.

## Break it two more ways

Send this to your AI partner:

> Take my pro-forma and break it in two ways the video did not show: first, make depreciation
> forget to reduce the property line; second, make the buyback reduce cash but not equity. For
> each, tell me before running what the check block will show and why, then run it and compare.

Judge each prediction against what printed. The fingerprint of an error is the point, not the fix.

## Earn a fourth history ratio

Pick one assumption you labelled judgment and go back to the filings for three more years. Does the
longer history change the label? Write the two sentences that would go under it.

## The loss-maker

If your company has negative free cash flow to equity, ask your AI what a valuation would need in
order to be meaningful: a year the cash flow turns positive, and what has to be true for it. Write
the conditional sentence a portfolio manager could act on.
