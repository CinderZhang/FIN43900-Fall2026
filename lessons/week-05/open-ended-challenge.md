# Optional Open-Ended Challenge — Make the Error Cost Explicit

Assign a plausible relative cost to a false approval and a false rejection. Recalibrate one
threshold to minimize total decision cost rather than maximize accuracy. Explain which cost is
hardest to estimate, which stakeholder bears it, and how the policy changes if portfolio
capacity or economic conditions change. Close AI and defend why the numerically cheapest policy
may still violate a mandate or fairness/consistency requirement.

Record the original policy, revised policy, expected and actual error tradeoff, stakeholder impact,
and conditional committee action so another analyst can challenge the choice.
