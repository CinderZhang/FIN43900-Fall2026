# Private Return — An Invitation After Major Work

This is for you. **Do not submit it.** It is not graded, collected, tracked, or analyzed.

Take five to ten minutes after a project or the capstone. Write, sketch, record a private voice
note, walk without recording anything, or simply think. Use any prompts that are useful:

- What do I understand now that I could not explain before?
- What did I merely repeat, complete, or make look finished?
- What surprised me when the evidence or model resisted my first story?
- Which assumption did I protect for too long?
- When did AI expand my judgment, and when did it let me avoid thinking?
- What decision would I make differently now? What evidence could still reverse it?
- What practice, question, or standard do I want to carry into my next piece of work?

Keep, revise, or delete what you make. You owe the course no disclosure. Choosing not to do this
has no effect on your grade. The invitation exists because some of the most consequential learning
cannot be responsibly inspected or scored by an instructor.
