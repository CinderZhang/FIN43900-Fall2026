# Lab 19 — Signal Decision

**Category:** Thursday merit checkout, 0–25 points.

For any criterion, use these intermediate anchors: **4** = sound with one minor,
non-load-bearing weakness; **2** = relevant evidence but an important link is incomplete;
**1** = minimal relevant attempt; **0** = missing, inaccessible, nonresponsive, or fabricated.

| Criterion | 5 — strong observable evidence | 3 — usable but incomplete | 0–1 — absent or unreliable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decision and research design | user/action, frozen rule, benchmark, holdout, and failure threshold explicit | one design boundary vague | goal changed after results |
| Data/evidence integrity | point-in-time lag and complete selection ledger preserved | minor disclosure gap | leakage or hidden search |
| Validation and financial soundness | gross/net, costs, subperiod, placebo, uncertainty, and capacity interpreted | several valid but shallow checks | in-sample chart or AI agreement only |
| Investment judgment | reject/redesign/more evidence/bounded research pilot follows evidence and mechanism; claim limits clear | usable action with vague condition | capital adoption or failed holdout reversed post hoc |
| Explanation and transfer | bull/bear dispositions, monitoring, kill rule, and new-hypothesis boundary defended | mostly descriptive | copied narrative/no ownership |

Checkout: Lab Date · teammates/learning partners/contribution · frozen rule and selection ledger · robustness table ·
cost/uncertainty/capacity conventions · mechanism and unsupported speculation · AI dispositions ·
action/range/monitor/kill rule · no-AI reversal answer · optional link · session token (announced in the room; it verifies your attendance declaration) · in-person attendance declaration · attestation · receipt.

End your checkout with a **growth note** before you submit — two or three sentences,
**ungraded and for you alone**: what can you do now that you could not do before this
lab, and what still feels shaky? Nobody scores this. It is your own record of becoming
good at this, and by December it is the raw material your project Reflect videos are
built from.
