Build — your skill and your app, your request

Minutes 15–40

No request is given: write your own to your AI from this list, Buffett's page and the rules.

What a skill is: a folder with a SKILL.md file. The file starts with a name and a description between --- lines, then gives your AI the instructions. Scripts can sit in the same folder.

Your skill must:

  1. live where your AI reads skills (its skills page says where), with a name and a description that says when to use it;
  2. bundle a script that, for a ticker, downloads the company's facts from SEC EDGAR — keyless, with a User-Agent naming you and your email — and prints the last five fiscal years: revenue, net income, operating cash flow, capital spending, equity, ROE and owner earnings, each with its XBRL tag;
  3. compute Buffett's three checks in code: ROE of 15% or more every year; owner earnings positive every year; owner earnings grew, first year to last;
  4. then have the AI, as Buffett, write a short memo — own the business, watch it, or pass — using only numbers the script printed, saved as a .md file. The memo judges the business, not the price: no price data this week.

Your app must: a Streamlit app that, for a ticker,

  1. shows owner earnings and net income over the five years as a chart;
  2. shows the table and the three checks;
  3. shows the memo, and flags every number in the memo the script did not print;
  4. says under the title: data from SEC EDGAR; built with AI help, a learning demonstration, not investment advice.

Sources: your AI's skills page — Antigravity · Codex · SEC EDGAR: ticker to CIK, https://www.sec.gov/files/company_tickers.json; facts, https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK##########.json (the CIK padded to ten digits); EDGAR API docs · Buffett's page · Streamlit docs.

Ask your AI to use your skill on COST. Expect: Costco's five fiscal years with their tags, the three checks, and Buffett's memo in a .md file. Then run the app. Expect: owner earnings and net income both up from FY2022 to FY2026, the table, the checks and the memo.
