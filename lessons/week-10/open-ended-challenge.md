# Open-Ended Challenge — The Revised Surprise

A vendor republishes last month after correcting 8% of records but supplies only the latest file.
The revised signal predicts returns.

1. Without AI, separate what is historically knowable from what exists only today.
2. Ask AI to propose a historical repair. Identify every step that assumes an unavailable vintage.
3. Design the minimum admissible alternative: obtain timestamped vintages, use an independently
   archived source, or preregister a prospective test.
4. Close AI and recommend proceed, wait, prospective-only, or reject.

Deliver a short protocol with information set, evidence request, test start, frozen rule, claim
limit, and reversal condition. “Backfill the corrected file” is not a point-in-time solution.
