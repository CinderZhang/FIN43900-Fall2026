# Lab 17 — Point-in-Time Audit

**Category:** Thursday merit checkout, 0–25 points.

For any criterion, use these intermediate anchors: **4** = sound with one minor,
non-load-bearing weakness; **2** = relevant evidence but an important link is incomplete;
**1** = minimal relevant attempt; **0** = missing, inaccessible, nonresponsive, or fabricated.

| Criterion | 5 — strong observable evidence | 3 — usable but incomplete | 0–1 — absent or unreliable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decision definition | historical user, cutoff, permitted action, and label boundary explicit | one boundary vague | no decision timestamp |
| Data/evidence integrity | event/availability/revision/identity/duplicate facts trace by row | minor unresolved field | silent deletion or invented provenance |
| Validation | reproducible as-of rule, naive comparison, and changed-cutoff/revision test interpreted | valid but shallow comparison | event-date join or AI agreement only |
| Market judgment | proceed/quarantine/prospective/reject follows the evidence and license boundary | action with vague condition | useful correlation overrides leakage |
| Explanation/transfer | AI dispositions, limitation, monitoring, and reversal condition are defensible | mostly descriptive | copied table with no ownership |

Checkout: Lab Date · teammates/learning partners/contribution · frozen leakage prediction · as-of and revision rule ·
admitted/quarantined row IDs · naive-versus-valid difference · changed-time test · AI red-team
dispositions · decision/condition/reversal trigger · optional link · session token (announced in the room; it verifies your attendance declaration) · in-person attendance declaration, truth attestation · receipt.

End your checkout with a **growth note** before you submit — two or three sentences,
**ungraded and for you alone**: what can you do now that you could not do before this
lab, and what still feels shaky? Nobody scores this. It is your own record of becoming
good at this, and by December it is the raw material your project Reflect videos are
built from.
