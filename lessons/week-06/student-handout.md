## Covenant sheet

For each covenant record numerator, denominator, permitted adjustments, period, test date,
threshold, headroom, source paragraph, cure/waiver provisions, and consequence. Labels such as
“leverage” or “coverage” are insufficient.

Training-case thresholds:

- maximum Debt/EBITDA: 5.00x;
- minimum EBIT/interest: 2.50x; and
- minimum cash/liquidity: USD 50 million.

These are synthetic course terms, not a claim about a real borrower.

## Causal scenario map

`demand/price/volume → revenue → margin → EBITDA → EBIT → CFO/liquidity`

`rates/refinancing → cash interest → coverage → cash/liquidity`

`cash burn/new borrowing → debt → leverage → covenant headroom`

Write the mechanism before changing cells. Name interactions and avoid counting the same shock
twice.

## Decision states

- **model flag:** screen or test requests attention;
- **covenant breach:** stated contractual test is not met;
- **payment default:** required payment/obligation is not met under the contract;
- **economic loss:** lender/investor value is impaired; and
- **cure/waiver:** contractual response that may not restore economics.
