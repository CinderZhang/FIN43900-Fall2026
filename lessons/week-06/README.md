# Week 6 — Pro-Forma Financial Modeling II: Take It Apart, Then Present It


## Mission

Tuesday: the same model, taken apart — every assumption shocked one at a time, one named downside
case, and the question that turns a model into an argument: what would have to be true for today's
price to be right? Thursday: put it in front of people — the two-line message, the one page, the
eight questions, and a partner who plays the sceptic.

## You arrive with

- your company's pro-forma from Lab 10, balancing, on GitHub;
- your labelled assumption set with the partner's attack answered;
- Part 2 of the pro-forma video, watched (prework below);
- a new partner.

## Outcomes

- Rerun a model under one-at-a-time shocks and one named case, and explain why the case is not
  the sum of the shocks.
- Write a locked prediction before changing an input, then reconcile it with the result.
- Say what the market price would require of one assumption, in operating language.
- Deliver the two-line message and the one page, and answer the eight standard questions with a
  number each.

## The two sessions

- **Session 11** — take it apart. Checkout: [Lab 11](lab-11-proforma-what-if.md).
- **Session 12** — present and defend. Checkout: [Lab 12](lab-12-proforma-present.md).

Dates and points: the course schedule and Brightspace.

## Teams this week

New partner on Tuesday; two companies per team. Tuesday you exchange locked predictions before
either of you reruns anything. Thursday your partner is the sceptic who asks the two hardest of
the eight questions. Checkouts stay individual.

## Prerequisites and prework

**Before Tuesday — about 40 minutes.**

1. Watch [Pro-Forma Valuation with AI, Part 2 — Take it apart, and present it](https://youtu.be/zDBUpjaQzpo)
   (22 min). Open the [Part 2 slides](../week-05/pro-forma-abg-tutorial.md) alongside; press **N** for
   the spoken text.
2. **Answer:** which two assumptions will move your company's value most, and which way does a
   one-point change move it? Write it down without running anything. This is your first locked prediction.
3. Reopen your Course/Work Folder and run your pro-forma. *Expect:* Lab 10's values.

**Before Thursday — about 20 minutes.**

1. Draft the two-line message for your company, the shape from Part 2: value and price, what drives
   the gap, what breaks it, where you stand. Bring it; Thursday you make it defensible.

## DRIVER this week

Define: the same question for everyone — which assumptions carry your value, and what would have to
be true for the price to be right? Represent: the shock list and one named case. Implement: one
request that reruns the model. Validate: the ABG known answers, then your locked prediction against
the result. Evolve: the case, the price-implied solve, the one page. Reflect: with the sceptic.
