# Week 6 — Pro-Forma Sensitivity: Find and Explain the Drivers

## Mission

Use your own company's pro-forma to discover which assumptions move its results.
Then explain the mechanism and test whether your conclusion depends on the ranges you chose.

## You arrive with

- your company model and assumption table from Lab 10;
- your saved base results and accounting checks;
- the same AI chat, ready to resume.

## Outcomes

- Compare operating drivers by changing one independent input at a time.
- Trace an input through the statements to operating profit, cash flow and, when valid, value.
- Explain how the tested ranges affect a driver ranking.
- Choose an assumption to research using both its impact and uncertainty.

## Prerequisites and prework

**Before Tuesday — about 20 minutes.**

1. Read the [worked sensitivity example](student-handout.md). The written handout is sufficient
   preparation; its teaching example does not need to be rerun.
2. Reopen your Course/Work Folder using **VS Code → File → Open Recent**, then **Terminal → New
   Terminal**. Run your company model with the command from Lab 10. *Expect:* your saved base results.
3. **Answer:** which two operating assumptions might drive your model’s results, and why?
   Locate them in your model. Bring any questions about their units or links to the statements.

For additional context, the sensitivity sections of [Pro-Forma Valuation with AI, Part 2](https://youtu.be/zDBUpjaQzpo)
illustrate the method. Use this week's worksheets for the work to do.

**Before Thursday — about 15 minutes.**

1. Reopen your Lab 11 sensitivity results and prediction. Be ready to explain the main driver
   over your tested ranges.
2. Revisit the company sources behind those ranges. Identify the range you are least sure of.

## DRIVER this week

Define: which assumptions drive my company's results? Represent: inputs, units and justified
ranges. Implement: AI adds sensitivity analysis to the existing model. Validate: check the
accounting, differences and restored base. Evolve: revise a range and interpret the comparison.
Reflect: explain the mechanism and the next research priority to a partner.
