# Open-Ended Challenge — Honest Failure Memo

The strategy fails its untouched holdout but reveals one potentially useful operational insight.

1. Without AI, write the strongest claim the evidence still supports and three claims it cannot.
2. Ask AI for reject, redesign, and pilot cases; tag each statement as output, source, assumption,
   or speculation.
3. Design the minimum new/prospective evidence required for a redesigned hypothesis.
4. Close AI and recommend reject, redesign, or a tightly bounded pilot with size, duration,
   monitoring owner, and kill rule.

Deliver a one-page committee memo that preserves the failed result. A tuned success narrative is
weaker than an honest rejection with a decision-relevant next test.
