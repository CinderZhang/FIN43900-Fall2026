# Week 12 Studio Guide — Project 2 Q&A and Consultation

*Both Week 12 meetings are open Q&A/consultation studios. There is no lab checkout and no lab
points this week — bring your Project 2 work and leave with answers. The instructor is in the room
as your knowledge expert; your partner is your first cold reviewer.*

## How a studio session works

1. Come with your repository open on GitHub and one specific question, blocker, or claim to
   pressure-test.
2. Short whole-room open: common blockers we are seeing, answered once for everyone.
3. Pairs: the [handout's](student-handout.md) Tuesday cold run or Thursday rehearsal, then work.
4. Open consultation: a first-come queue on the whiteboard. Answers that matter to everyone are
   re-broadcast to the room.

## Before you ask (optional, ungraded)

- State your committee decision and the claim most likely to reverse it.
- Check the [handout's table](student-handout.md#where-each-project-2-part-comes-from): every
  Project 2 part has a lab behind it.
- Run your partner's README cold; note the first failure and the time.

Project 2 requirements live on [the Project 2 page](../../projects/project-2-investment-management-application.md);
submission mechanics and the deadline are on the Brightspace assignment.
