# Week 12 Studio Guide — Project 2 Q&A and Consultation

*Both Week 12 meetings are open Q&A/consultation studios. There is no lab checkout and no lab
points this week — bring your Project 2 work and leave with answers. The instructor and TAs
are in the room as your knowledge experts.*

## How a studio session works

1. Come with your project open and one specific question, blocker, or claim to pressure-test.
2. Short whole-room open: common blockers we are seeing, answered once for everyone.
3. Open consultation: work, ask, verify. First-come queue on the whiteboard.
4. Answers that matter to everyone get re-broadcast to the room.

## Recommended self-audit before you ask (optional, ungraded)

- State your committee decision and the highest-risk claim behind it.
- List three prioritized failure modes across finance/mandate, data/timing, and
  technical/reproducibility.
- Complete a README-only cold run and note the first failure and elapsed time.
- Walk your mandate, point-in-time, baseline, holdout, selection-ledger, cost/capacity, and
  constraint checks; ask AI for one counterexample and verify it independently.
- Repair, qualify, or reject — and freeze your must-fix list with owners.

## Recommended defense rehearsal (optional, ungraded)

Practice the two-minute defense with a partner and take one skeptical question. The Project 2
rubric criteria are the probes: evidence evolution, technical ownership, validation,
investment judgment, response quality.
