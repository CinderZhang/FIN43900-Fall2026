# Project 2 — Investment Management Application

You are a portfolio analyst. Build an investment management app a committee could adopt, and
recommend **adopt, allocate to, rebalance, pilot, or reject** your process under your mandate.

Points and the enforced due date appear in the Brightspace assignment header. Edition A and
Edition B are yours to keep and compare; neither is submitted. Use AI from day one. How you build it is yours.

## Base requirements

Every project meets all eight.

1. **The app runs.** A Python web app a stranger starts from your README and operates without
   you. A notebook or a script that computes once is not an app.
2. **Your mandate** from Lab 13: objective, universe, benchmark, risk measure and limits.
3. **Two baselines and your portfolio.** Equal weight and a second transparent baseline beside
   your constrained portfolio, each with its mandate verdict (pass or fail, and which rule) in
   the app.
4. **One point-in-time signal** with its data card.
5. **A stress run** on estimates, window, costs and constraints, and the condition that stops
   the process.
6. **One Locked Changed-Input Record:** your AI-off prediction, committed to GitHub before the
   run, then the result.
7. **A recommendation** with an allocation range and the triggers you would monitor.
8. **Two statements** open your README, your memo and each video: which AI you used and for
   what, and that the analysis and the decision are yours; education, not investment advice.

## Submit

1. The GitHub repository.
2. `docs/decision-memo.md`.
3. `docs/validation-and-ai-use.md`, with the Locked Changed-Input Record.
4. Three videos as YouTube links, not video files, with transcripts enabled: Video 1, how
   your system and your thinking changed, and one AI output you checked; Video 2, the code
   walkthrough; Video 3, the committee presentation, opening with your app running live. Attach
   all the transcripts, or link the transcripts in your repo.
5. The manifest: every location a GitHub link, except the videos.

Attach the same `.md` files to the Brightspace submission. Video lengths and the manifest's rows:
the [student checklist](major-project-student-checklist.md).

## Grading

The 300-point mapping in the major-project analytic rubric applies, graded strictly: Decision-ready
goes only to an app a committee could adopt. An honest reject of a failed process is fully
responsive.
