# Formative Studio — Recommendation Control Test

## Evidence packet

Retain, but do not submit to a grade item:

1. pre-AI scenario predictions and frozen Base result;
2. candidate-by-limit matrices for all three scenarios;
3. expected/actual direction reconciliation;
4. 0.25% versus 0.90% ten-year fee-drag calculation and convention limits;
5. provider-compensation conflict question and evidence request;
6. two named AI recommendations with claim-level dispositions;
7. independently verified candidate/reason codes, fee result, and one narrative claim;
8. retain/change/defer action plus a client explanation; and
9. monitor, owner, frequency, and two reversal/escalation triggers.

## Adversarial test

One reviewer plays the provider product lead and argues for the highest-compensation candidate.
One plays the client advocate and attacks fees, cash needs, and certainty. The analyst must answer
both using the same frozen record. Demeanor, confidence, and persuasive polish are not evidence.

## Stop rule

If no candidate is admissible, stop automated recommendation. Record the failed constraints,
missing alternatives, human escalation owner, and evidence needed to resume. “No recommendation”
is a valid control output.
