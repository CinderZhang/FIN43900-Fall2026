# Open Challenge — The Conflicted Explanation

An AI recommends IncomePlus because its synthetic net-return input exceeds Balanced and the
provider earns more compensation on it. The narrative calls the portfolio “safe income” and does
not mention fees, illiquidity, or the client's cash floor.

First work without AI. Build the strongest evidence-based case for deeper review, then list every
hard-constraint breach, unsupported word, omitted fact, and conflict question. Calculate the
ten-year difference between 0.25% and 0.90% fees under the supplied convention. Decide whether to
reject, defer, or investigate; do not infer misconduct from compensation alone.

Then ask AI to repair the explanation without changing the case facts. Accept, modify, or reject
each material edit. Finally introduce the LiquidityShock scenario. Predict the direction, run the
screen, and explain why an empty admissible set should trigger human redesign rather than an
automatic constraint override. Close with the evidence that would reopen the process.
