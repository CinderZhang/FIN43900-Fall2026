# Formative Studio — Mandate Before Model

## Evidence packet

Submit nothing to a grade item. Retain one dated packet containing:

1. the pre-AI client-fact authority table and admissibility prediction;
2. a mandate with objective, horizon, constraints, unknowns, and escalation owners;
3. a candidate-by-constraint pass/fail/unknown matrix with reason codes;
4. net-return calculations for admissible candidates only;
5. two named AI mandate interpretations and accept/modify/reject/escalate dispositions;
6. one independently verified AI claim and one rejected or qualified claim;
7. a conditional deeper-review/defer decision; and
8. monitoring and two reversal/escalation triggers.

## Acceptance standard

Strong evidence preserves the difference between willingness and capacity, uses the supplied
limits exactly, refuses to optimize infeasible candidates, exposes missing facts, and makes a
decision that follows the record. A polished persona, confident language, unexplained score, or
AI consensus is not evidence.

## Cold-review test

Give only the packet to a peer. In three minutes the peer must locate the decision, client facts,
constraints, admissible set, AI dispositions, unresolved unknown, and next action. Any item the
peer cannot locate is a repair, not a presentation problem.
